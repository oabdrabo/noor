Search the Qur'an & Hadith by meaning

Noor is AI semantic search over the Qur'an and Hadith. Ask in Arabic or English — "verses about patience in adversity", "حديث عن حقوق الجار" — and get matches by meaning, not keyword, with cited and grounded answers.

Grounded Q&A — a synthesised answer with every claim cited to the retrieved verses & hadith.

What it does

Semantic search

Arabic or English, across the Qur'an and major Hadith collections — ranked by meaning, not surface form.

Grounded Q&A

Retrieves the most relevant passages, then writes a cited answer drawn from only those sources.

Voice search

Speak a question; it's transcribed and run through whichever mode you're in.

Reference lookup

A reference like 2:255 resolves straight to the verse.

Topic discovery

TF-IDF and clustering surface concepts and themes across the corpus.

Light & dark

An Arabic-first interface that stays calm to read in either theme.

How it's built

Multilingual embeddings in a sqlite-vec vector store — the entire index is a single SQLite file, deployable on hardware as small as a Raspberry Pi. A FastAPI backend, a single-file Vue 3 frontend, and nginx at the edge. Q&A and voice are reached server-side, so no model keys ever reach the browser.
